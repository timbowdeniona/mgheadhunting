## 3. What Has Been Holding the Site Back (In Plain English)

None of the issues below are problems with your service or reputation — they are "under-the-bonnet" plumbing items from launch that we found during this check:

1. **Behind-the-Scenes Labels Pointing to `.com` Instead of `.co.uk`**  
   Websites carry invisible "business cards" that tell Google and AI tools your official web address and email. Those background labels were still pointing to `mgheadhunting.com` rather than `mgheadhunting.co.uk`. Because `mgheadhunting.com` belongs to an unrelated recruitment company in Mexico, Google and AI tools were getting mixed signals. Switching every background label to `mgheadhunting.co.uk` fixes this completely.
2. **Google Only Has Half Your Pages on File So Far**  
   Right now, Google has listed your Homepage and 11 articles, **but hasn't listed your FAQ, About, Sectors, Retained Search Process, or Contact pages yet** because the automatic "directory list" Google reads had a fault in it. Fixing that directory list and handing it directly to Google will get your whole site listed.
3. **Broken "Related Articles" Links & Date Typos on Insights**  
   At the bottom of individual Insight articles, the two *"Related Market Intelligence"* boxes currently lead to a dead link, and several published articles are accidentally dated **"September 2027"** instead of **2026**. In addition, the main `/insights` page still shows the old *"2026/2027 Executive Salary Benchmark"* box that we previously removed from the Homepage.
4. **Long Page Titles Getting Cut Off on Google**  
   Although 6 of your Case Studies sit on Page 1 of Google, almost nobody has clicked them yet because `"MG Headhunting"` is being stamped twice at the end of every page title — cutting off the useful part of the headline on Google's results screen.
5. **Small Inconsistencies in Numbers & Location Across Different Pages**  
   A few pages still have early template figures that don't quite match the candid, first-person answers you wrote on your FAQ page (for instance, *"12-month warranty"* on one page vs your real *"6-month free replacement"* on the FAQ page, or *"London & Home Counties"* in the footer vs *"West Midlands"* on the Contact page). Making every page match your real numbers helps both clients and AI tools trust every word.
6. **Two Missing Lines on the Website Enquiry Popup**  
   When someone fills out the *"Start the Conversation"* popup form, the boxes for **Role Title** and **Timeframe** weren't being included in the background message, and our test link version of the website was still visible alongside the live `.co.uk` site.
